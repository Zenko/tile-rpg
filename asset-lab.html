Try new art in the real game's clothes.

Drop your images in below and they are swapped into the game's own card faces, sizes, rarities, screens and town, right here in your browser. The real game is never changed. Name a file after a card id (sprout.png) or a sprite id (s-oak.svg) and it attaches to the right thing by itself; anything else can be assigned by hand.

1. Library

Add as many files as you like, in one go or over several visits. They stay in this browser until you remove them.

Drop images here or click to choose
PNG 512 x 512 transparent for cards. SVG or PNG for town sprites. You can drop a whole folder's worth at once.

2. One card, every way

Pick a card (the ones with new art are marked). Everything below shows that card with its new art, using the real card faces. Use the Dark / Light switch at the top to check both themes, and the Shadow switch to compare with and without the faint drop shadow the game adds to art itself.

3. In the game

The selected card dropped into mock-ups built from the game's real components, surrounded by the other cards in their current look (emoji, or your other uploads). This is the "does it fit?" check.

4. Whole set

Every card at once, with your uploads in place and the rest still showing today's emoji, so you can judge consistency across a family or rarity.

5. Keyword icons

The 13 small rule icons shown on every card (Guard, Swift, Mend and so on). Name a file kw-guard.png (or just guard.png) and it replaces that emoji everywhere in this page: the card faces, the mock screens and the lists. Aim for 128 x 128 px, transparent, a single bold shape. On a card each one sits inside a round badge (about 22% of the card width, filled pale in the Dark theme and charcoal in the Light theme) at about 55% of the badge, so it is shown at only 8 to 20 px. Make sure it reads on both circles: mid-tone, saturated colours, no pure white or black fills, subject inside the central 70%. Note: the game itself still draws these as emoji; once you are happy, I add image support to the game, as was done for card art.

6. All icons

Every icon the game uses, found by scanning its code: different ones, from the dock tabs to the smallest list bullet. Click any icon to replace it. A replacement is swapped in everywhere that icon appears, so one image restyles every screen. Name a file icon-<name>.png (the name is shown on each icon, for example icon-wrapped-present.png), or use the Upload button on an icon. Aim for 128 x 128 px, transparent, bold and simple: most are shown at 12 to 24 px.

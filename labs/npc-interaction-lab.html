Nine ways the screen you get when you tap a neighbour could work, all playable with the same pretend neighbour, so you can compare feel and not just looks. 1 is the screen in the game today. Every option has the same four jobs: Chat, Play, Gift and Trade. Switch the neighbour to a boss to see the rules we just added (no chat or gifts, and Trade locked until they are beaten). On a computer, the two classic ones also take the arrow keys, Enter and Esc.

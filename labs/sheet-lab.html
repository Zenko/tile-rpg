Sheet Lab

One shared sheet kit for every interactive screen: a header that says where you are, one obvious primary action, secondary choices as rows with an icon tile and a line of help, locked things that show progress, and a sticky footer for the way out. Sheets fit their content so the world stays visible. Everything here is tappable.

What changed, and why

  • Hierarchy. Today every choice is the same grey slab, and the one highlighted button (the mini-game) is the only thing with a colour. Now there is one primary action per sheet, then quieter rows, then the exit.
  • Each row explains itself. A 44px icon tile, a bold title and one line of help ("2 boxes thirsty", "Needs 5 hearts · you have 2"). Counts and rewards move into pills or the help line instead of floating in the row.
  • Locked is a state, not a dead button. Fenn's ranked match shows a progress bar toward the 5 hearts it needs.
  • Sheets fit their content. The bonsai sheet had 60% empty space; it now peeks up and leaves the plant and its glow visible. Tall lists (favourites, letters) get a header and a sticky footer so the action never scrolls away.
  • Bubbles sit above the sheet, not on the character. The cottage speech used to cover the gardener's hat; now it is anchored to the sheet edge.
  • Close is always in the same place. A round ✕ top-right on lists and detail sheets; "Head back out" stays at the foot of a building. "Not now" and "Close" text rows are gone.
  • Pickers show state. Favourites shows three frame slots that fill as you choose, a filled star and number on picked cards, the rest dimming at 3.
  • Letters are one tap to expand, the gift is an attachment inside the letter with its own Claim button, and Claim all lives in the footer.

If you want it in the game

  • Building choices: restyle #sceneView.sc-std .sc-btn in css/latest.css, and have sceneBtn() in js/houses-and-cellar.js emit .ico, .tx (title and help) and .go spans.
  • Other sheets: .overlay.sheet .overlay-card gets the header, body and footer structure; padding at the bottom of the card is already safe-area aware.
  • Keep the existing motion: sheet rise, then rows staggering in by --n, all transform and opacity, off under reduced motion.

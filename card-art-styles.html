Card Art Styles

The same six cards and thirteen keyword icons, redrawn in full colour. Every style keeps what the emoji do well: colour fills, gradients, highlights and soft shadows. They differ only in the surface finish. The layout is the game’s own. The first row is what you have now.

    The drawings are quick placeholders made to compare finishes. Every style uses the same underlying vector art, so the finish can be swapped later without redrawing. The ⚔ and ♥ stat marks are left as they are in every row.

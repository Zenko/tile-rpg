Card Art Styles

Nine finishes in total: your two favourites first, then seven new ones. Every row shows six everyday cards and the four divine cards, plus all 13 keyword icons. The layout is the game’s own, and the art is full colour with gradient fills and soft shadows.

    The drawings are quick placeholders made to compare finishes. Every style uses the same underlying vector art, so the finish can be swapped later without redrawing. The ⚔ and ♥ stat marks are left as they are in every row.

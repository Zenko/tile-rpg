Card Art Styles

The same six cards and thirteen keyword icons, drawn seven ways. The layout is the game's own: cost badge, art, keyword row, name, power and calm. Only the art and the finish change. The first row is what you have now.

    All art here is placeholder vector drawing, made to compare directions. Real card art would be drawn per card, and there are 150+ cards, so the style you pick also decides how much work each new card takes. Glyph, line and paper cut are the cheapest to extend. Pixel and crayon need a hand-drawn pass per card. The ⚔ and ♥ stat marks are left as they are in every row.

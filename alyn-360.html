1. Turntable

Drag the figure sideways, or use the slider. 24 turn angles at 3 camera heights.

0°
0° is facing you. If she turns the wrong way when you drag, press "Swap left/right" (it also applies to the walking test).

2. Walking test

Arrow keys or WASD (diagonals work), or tap anywhere to walk there. Her facing comes from the heading, using the 0° height row.

Accessory: bow

A first accessory test. The bow is drawn from a small 3D model at each of the 24 angles, so it turns, shrinks edge-on and goes behind her head with her. Shown at eye level (camera 0°); it works in both tests below.

Accessory: hat

A sun hat with a ribbon band, or a cap with a bill. Both sit on a ring around her head, so the brim and the bill are drawn in front of or behind the crown depending on the angle. With a hat on, the hair bow is hidden under it.

Accessory: glasses

Each lens is placed on the curve of her face and only drawn while it faces you, so from the side you see one narrow lens and the arm, and from behind just the ends of the arms.

1. Turntable

Drag the figure sideways, or use the slider. 24 turn angles at 3 camera heights.

0°
0° is facing you. If she turns the wrong way when you drag, press "Swap left/right" (it also applies to the walking test).

2. Walking test

Arrow keys or WASD (diagonals work), or tap anywhere to walk there. Her facing comes from the heading, using the 0° height row.
